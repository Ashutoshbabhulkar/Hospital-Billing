Eka Care Chrome Extension Setup Instructions

The Eka Care Billing Extractor extension allows you to capture patient details directly from the Eka Care portal (connect.ekacare.com) with a single click.

1

Open Chrome Extensions Page

In Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions or click Extensions Menu > Manage Extensions.

2

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

3

Load Unpacked Extension

Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the folder:

d:\Softwares\Surgery bill\extension
4

Capture Patient Data on Eka Care

Open any patient EMR or record page on Eka Care. You will see a floating button "Generate Hospital Bill" at the bottom-right of the page, or click the extension popup to extract details instantly into this Billing Agent!
